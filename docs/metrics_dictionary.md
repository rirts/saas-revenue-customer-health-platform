# Metrics

- Current MRR: active or past-due subscription MRR.
- MRR at risk: current MRR on accounts matching the configured risk rules.
- Usage: events in the inclusive window ending at `as_of_date` (90 days by default).
- Critical tickets: unresolved critical tickets created on/before the observation date.
- Revenue retention mart: current revenue exposure by segment; it does not calculate historical NRR/GRR.
- Health score: bounded rule-based score; thresholds are demo assumptions.
